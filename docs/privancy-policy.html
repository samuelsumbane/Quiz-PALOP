Privacy Policy — PALOP Quiz

PALOP Quiz respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the app. Information Collection

The PALOP Quiz does not collect, store, or transmit users’ personal information.

The app does not require:

  • Account creation
  • Name or email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Sending text messages
  • Access to the device's contacts

Answers provided in the quiz and the user’s progress are processed locally on the device.


Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services, such as advertising services, which may collect certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

If ads are used, they may be provided by partners such as Google AdMob, which has its own Privacy Policy.

Security

Although we do not collect personal data, we recommend that users keep their operating system and the app up to date for better security.
